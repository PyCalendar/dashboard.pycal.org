============
Your account
============

Signing in is optional. It adds a private page, *Account*, with your preferences
and what waits for you.

Sign in
=======

1. Press **Sign in with GitHub** at the top.
2. GitHub asks whether the dashboard may know who you are and read your email
   addresses. Accept.
3. You return to the page *Account*.

There are no passwords on the dashboard. It stores no token of yours and never
writes anything to GitHub on your behalf.

The page Account
================

Only you see it. It shows:

- **Email addresses**: those GitHub shared, whether they are verified and which
  one is primary. *Add, verify or change email addresses* adds another one,
  which the dashboard verifies with a link.
- **Preferences**:

  - *Email me a weekly summary of what waits for me*: off by default. The site
    only writes to verified addresses.
  - *Show draft pull requests*.
  - *Period of the statistics*: 30, 90 or 365 days.
  - *Repositories I watch*: the start page shows them first, with their numbers.

- **My open pull requests** and **Asked to review**.

Your public page
================

*My public activity* in the menu of your account opens your page under
**Contributors**. It shows the same public data as GitHub.

Sign out
========

Choose **Sign out** in the menu of your account.
