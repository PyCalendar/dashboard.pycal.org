=================
Use the dashboard
=================

The dashboard guides the contributor community of PyCalendar. Its automation
is designed next. The site:

- lists projects, sets of repositories with their open issues and pull
  requests, which people create and share, see :doc:`projects`,
- signs people in with GitHub, with an account page for the preferences and
  the email addresses, see :doc:`account`,
- lets people install its GitHub App on repositories, see
  :doc:`/how-to/install-app`,
- receives the webhooks of the app and logs them for the administrators, and
  tells people when an installation needs more permissions. It writes nothing
  to GitHub yet.

Every page is public, except the account, the preferences, the email
addresses, the webhook deliveries, and the private projects, which only their
people see.

The start page
==============

Open `dashboard.pycal.org <https://dashboard.pycal.org>`_. The start page
welcomes you; the menu at the top leads to the projects, to signing in and to
your account, where you install the GitHub App. *About*, at
the bottom of every page, shows the permissions of the app, the version that
runs, and links the documentation and the code: the footer repeats none of it.

The language
============

The site speaks English, German and Spanish. It starts in the language of your
browser; the menu with the globe at the top changes it and keeps the choice.
Signed in, you choose it in your account for every device, see
:ref:`preferences`.
To help translate, see :ref:`translate`.

The colors
==========

The button with the sun and the moon switches between light, dark and the
colors of your system, like on sign.pycal.org. The site keeps the choice in a
cookie; it works without JavaScript. Signed in, the choice is kept in your
account for every device, see :ref:`preferences`.

When something goes wrong
=========================

An error page says what happened, gives details to copy and links to the
description of the error in :doc:`/reference/errors`, which says why it
appears and how to resolve it.
