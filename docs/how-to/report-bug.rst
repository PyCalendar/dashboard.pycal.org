====================================
Report a problem or give feedback
====================================

Open a `new issue with the form Problem or feedback
<https://github.com/PyCalendar/dashboard.pycal.org/issues/new?template=feedback.yml>`_
on GitHub, also from **About → Report a problem or leave feedback** and from
the page of an error. Fill in what you know; this is where to find it:

The address of the page
   Copy it from the address bar of the browser.

The details of an error
   On the page of the error, open *Details to copy* below the buttons, select
   the text and copy it. It names the error, the page, the time and the
   version.

The version
   At the bottom of each page, **About**, then the tab **Version**, such as
   ``v0.13.0``; also in ``/about.json``, ``build.version``.

What you did before
   The clicks and what you typed, one step a line, so that the error can be
   seen again.

Screenshots
   Take them with :kbd:`Print` or :kbd:`Shift+Print` on Windows and Linux,
   :kbd:`Shift+Command+4` on a Mac, or the buttons of a phone, and paste them
   into the form or drag them in. Cover what is private, such as email
   addresses.

The browser and the device
   Such as Firefox 140 on Ubuntu, or Safari on an iPhone.

Security problems are reported privately with a `security advisory
<https://github.com/PyCalendar/dashboard.pycal.org/security/advisories/new>`_.
